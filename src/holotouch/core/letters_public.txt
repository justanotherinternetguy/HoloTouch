letters_public.npz holds the hand landmarks of ASLNow!'s fingerspelling dataset, by sid220:

    https://huggingface.co/datasets/sid220/asl-now-fingerspelling
    revision 9b3c96ae0adb7744a2c9fc72692842e6b3e25e33

It is published under the MIT licence. The 1874 hands of the 24 letters that are not drawn in the
air are kept, each from its wrist, with x and depth scaled by a 4:3 picture. letters.npz is the
model trained on them. research/letters_public.py makes both files.
